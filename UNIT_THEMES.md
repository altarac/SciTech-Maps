# Unit colours

The shared map and reader theme uses the exact colours sampled from the owner's curriculum-planning slide, supplied 7 October 2026. Navy `#102b43` anchors the header and text. Light teaching surfaces preserve readability. The coloured unit title band, lesson markers, Learn area, vocabulary and flashcards share the selected unit's theme.

| Subject family | Slide colour | Grade/unit assignments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matter, energy and forces | Teal `#4ab8b2` | G3 U1–2; G4 U1; G5 U1–2; G6 U1 |
| Designed systems | Burnt orange `#c86f3b` | G6 U2 |
| Earth and space | Gold `#f1b34b` | G3 U3; G4 U2; G6 U3–4 |
| Living systems and human impact | Sage green `#72b08e` | G3 U4; G4 U3–4; G5 U3–4 |
| Shared library and planning | Slate blue `#829bb0` | Shared navigation/library shell |

Mixed units take the colour of their main storyline. G4 U1 remains teal for its forces/motion foundation; G6 U4 remains gold for its dynamic-Earth foundation. These are navigation cues, not new curriculum categories or changes to lesson content.

Dark companion colours are used for small text and buttons: teal `#166b67`, orange `#94471d`, gold `#79500d`, green `#32664a`, slate `#40586d`. The slide's brighter swatches are used with navy text rather than white text. Sentence-reading highlights retain their separate yellow reading-position role. Word chapters, illustrations and PowerPoint files are unchanged by this web-theme update.

`unit-theme.js` owns the 16 unit assignments; `unit-theme.css` owns the reusable colours and accessible component styling.
